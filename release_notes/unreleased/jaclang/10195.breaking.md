- **`select = ["all"]` now strips comments and docstrings and separates impls**: a new lint rule, `separate-impls` (W3055), asks that every function, method and ability body live in an `impl`; `jac fmt --lintfix` keeps the declaration in place and moves the body into an `impl` at the bottom of the module, in declaration order. Bodies an `impl` cannot name stay inline (nested functions, nameless abilities, generics, accessors, and names a field or another member shares). `select = ["all"]` now turns on `separate-impls`, `strip-comments` (W3050) and `strip-docstrings` (W3051); `select = ["default"]` still leaves all three off, and each can be named on its own or listed in `ignore`. The Jac tree itself has been moved to this layout. Alongside it, Python codegen stops wrapping same-file impls in `impl_patch_filename` (and so stops importing `jaclang.lib.jaclib` for them), E1095 follows `self.method()` calls made from `postinit`, and a dotted impl with no signature formats as `impl Foo.bar {` instead of `impl Foo.bar{`.
