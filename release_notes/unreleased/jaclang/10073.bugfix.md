- **Fix: SVG elements and the remaining HTML elements are intrinsic JSX tags**: `<circle>`, `<path>`, `<rect>`, `<g>`, `<defs>`, `<text>` and the other SVG elements, and HTML elements such as `<kbd>`, `<b>`, `<i>`, `<u>`, `<samp>` and `<var>`, were missing from the intrinsic-element registry, so each opening tag warned W1050 (unknown intrinsic element) and W2001 (name may be undefined).
