- **Fix: a dotted JSX tag checks its head, not its member parts as names**: in `<TooltipPrimitive.Provider>` the member part `Provider` was looked up as a free-standing name and warned W2001 and W1051, a member sharing a builtin's name was bound to that builtin, and a lowercase head (`<motion.div>`) was treated as an intrinsic element. A dotted tag is now typed as a member expression: only its head is resolved as a name.
