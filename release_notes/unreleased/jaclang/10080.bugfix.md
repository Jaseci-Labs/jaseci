- **Fix: `jac check` spent its time outside type checking**: validating cached comptime dependencies re-parsed large source files in every new process, and the garbage collector kept rescanning the compiler's own objects. Definition digests are now cached on disk, the check heap is frozen between files, and worker pools take the largest files first. Re-checking an edited 26-file package with two workers went from 11.2 s to 6.8 s.
