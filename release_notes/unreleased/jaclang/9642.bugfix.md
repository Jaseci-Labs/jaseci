- **Fix: A static `@schedule` task runs on one replica at a time**: a run now holds its task in the database (renewed every 10 seconds and expiring 30 seconds after the last renewal, so a worker that dies or loses the database gives it up), and a tick that arrives while any worker or replica holds it is skipped everywhere without being claimed. Before, a busy worker left the tick unclaimed and another replica ran the task alongside it.
