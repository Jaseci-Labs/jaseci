- **Fix: a `jac scale deploy` after an edit recompiles only the modules that changed**: every seal ran in a fresh stage, so precompile compiled the whole application again even after a one-line edit. The seal now starts from the previous sealed bundle's compiled modules (for the files that still ship) and precompile reuses whatever still matches its source and dependencies. On a 493-module app the precompile phase went from 355 s to 9 s after a one-module edit.
